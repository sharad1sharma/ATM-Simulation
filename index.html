📖 ATM Guide: Register, Login & Logout
1
How to Register (New Users):

Click "New User? Register" on the Welcome screen or Login screen. Enter your chosen Username, set a 4-digit PIN / Password, confirm it, and choose an initial deposit (e.g. ₹1,000). Your account is instantly saved!

2
How to Login:

Click "Start / Login" or click the Debit Card. Type your registered Username and PIN / Password, then click "Login". You will enter your account dashboard!

3
How to Perform Transactions:

From the left menu, select Check Balance, Withdraw Money, Deposit Money, Transfer Money, or Mini Statement. For security, withdrawals, transfers, and balance checks prompt for your 4-digit PIN.

4
How to Logout:

Whenever you finish, click "Logout" in the left sidebar menu, the top navigation bar, or the red "Logout ⏻" button on the dashboard. The ATM also automatically logs you out after 40 seconds of inactivity for your protection.
